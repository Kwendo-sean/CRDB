# Design System

## Visual thesis
A CRDB institutional command centre expressed through an editorial data-grid: near-black operational surfaces, exact hairline rules, oversized short statements, disciplined mono labels and one CRDB red signal colour. It borrows LocBot's technical pacing without cloning the product or obscuring CRDB ownership.

## Brand roles
CRDB leads the title lockup and event identity. Predictive Analytics Lab appears as the technology collaborator. Use supplied approved logos/assets when provided; until then use text lockups rather than fabricated marks.

## Tokens
- Ink `#070808`, elevated `#0e1010`, panel `#121515`
- Paper `#f2f3ef`, muted `#9ca3a0`, rule `#29302e`
- CRDB signal red `#df1f26`; success `#58d68d`; warning `#f0b44d`
- Display: condensed/system sans fallback; body: highly readable sans; metadata: monospace
- Radius: 0–4px for functional controls only; no capsule/pill card language
- Spacing follows an 8px base with generous 48–120px editorial chapter gaps

## Grammar
Numbered chapters, asymmetric 8/4 grids, horizontal rules, rectangular actions, compact uppercase status labels, tabular numerals and restrained grid textures. Use colour for state, not decoration.

## Participant surfaces
Mobile-first. Learning Pass keeps the QR at least 240 CSS px with a white quiet zone, high contrast, pass identity and four metrics. Quiz choices are full-width, minimum 52px targets with letters A–D and clear selected/submitted states. Errors always offer an explicit retry path.

## Admin surfaces
Dense but calm operational tables, keyboard-visible controls and state indicators. Scanner prioritises camera viewport, participant result and duplicate/override state. Projector leaderboards use very large type, no private IDs/email, and safe-area spacing.

## Motion/accessibility
Short rule/opacity transitions only; no content depends on animation. Respect reduced motion. Meet WCAG AA contrast, visible focus, semantic headings/status live regions, form labels, camera fallback input and non-colour state cues.

## Copy
Use precise system language: `ACCESS YOUR LEARNING PASS`, `REGISTRATION FOUND`, `CHECK-IN VERIFIED`, `HOT HOUR / LIVE`, `LEARNING XP`, `SYNC / READY`. Avoid generic conference enthusiasm and invented claims.
